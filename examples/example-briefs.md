# Example Briefs

These examples show the kind of creative direction this skill should help clarify before accompaniment starts.

## Example 1: Late-night R&B demo

Input direction:
- “I want something intimate, urban, late-night, not too glossy.”
- “More SZA / Brent emotional space than stadium pop.”
- “Need room for topline later.”

Locked brief:
- Style: contemporary alt-R&B accompaniment
- Tempo: 92-98 BPM
- Key: D minor or F minor
- Harmony: moody minor with accessible lift
- Palette: soft drums, warm keys, sub bass, sparse upper textures
- Finish level: songwriting demo with believable section contrast
- Deliverable: WAV plus stems and Logic import notes

## Example 2: Radio-facing pop/R&B backing track

Input direction:
- “I want it polished enough to pitch.”
- “The chorus should feel bigger, but not EDM.”
- “Think The Weeknd-adjacent drama with cleaner pop structure.”

Locked brief:
- Style: pop-forward R&B accompaniment
- Tempo: 98-108 BPM
- Key: minor center with bright chorus lift
- Harmony: pop-accessible loop with stronger hook movement
- Palette: wider drums, brighter synth framing, more defined transitions
- Finish level: near-finished backing track
- Deliverable: stereo mix and Logic-ready prep package

## Example 3: Sparse topline canvas

Input direction:
- “I only need a minimal bed for writing.”
- “Keep it emotional, expensive, and open.”
- “Do not overcrowd the lead space.”

Locked brief:
- Style: minimalist songwriter accompaniment
- Tempo: 80-96 BPM
- Key: chosen around artist comfort
- Harmony: restrained loop, unresolved feeling
- Palette: keys, bass, selective percussion, one signature texture
- Finish level: arrangement sketch
- Deliverable: stereo bounce and source notes

## Example 4: Sparse singer-songwriter sketch

Input direction:
- "I want a simple emotional bed that supports guitar or piano writing."
- "Keep the accompaniment intimate, not cinematic."
- "The arrangement should leave room for a vocal later without suggesting a melody."

Locked brief:
- Style: acoustic-leaning singer-songwriter accompaniment
- Tempo: 72-84 BPM
- Key: G major, E minor, or adjusted for vocal comfort
- Harmony: plainspoken folk-pop loop with suspended color tones
- Palette: muted acoustic guitar, soft piano, light brushed percussion, warm pad
- Finish level: writing sketch with a clear verse-to-chorus energy shape
- Deliverable: stereo bounce, section map, and Logic import notes

## Example 5: Sync bed instrumental cue

Input direction:
- "I need a 60-second underscore for a small brand film."
- "It should move forward without a distracting lead hook."
- "Give me something that can cut cleanly into 15, 30, and 60 second versions."

Locked brief:
- Style: cinematic pop instrumental bed
- Tempo: 100-112 BPM
- Key: C major or A minor
- Harmony: simple open progression with low melodic commitment
- Palette: muted piano pulse, restrained percussion, warm bass, soft motion textures
- Finish level: polished cue sketch with clean edit points
- Deliverable: 60-second WAV, 15/30/60 edit notes, stems, and Logic import notes

## Example 6: Trap-influenced pop demo

Input direction:
- "I want modern trap bounce, but still pop-safe."
- "The hook should lift without turning into an aggressive drop."
- "Keep space for topline and do not crowd the vocal range."

Locked brief:
- Style: trap-influenced pop accompaniment
- Tempo: 140-152 BPM with half-time feel
- Key: F minor or G minor
- Harmony: compact minor loop with a brighter hook variation
- Palette: tight 808, crisp hats, filtered keys, sparse synth texture, clean transition hits
- Finish level: demo-ready backing track with clear hook contrast
- Deliverable: stereo demo, drums/bass/music stems, section map, and Logic import notes

## Example 7: Upbeat pop/R&B chorus-lift demo

Input direction:
- "Make it brighter and more rhythmic than the late-night R&B examples."
- "The chorus should open up emotionally, but not become EDM."
- "I want a full accompaniment arc that can support toplining later."

Locked brief:
- Style: upbeat pop/R&B accompaniment
- Tempo: 108-118 BPM
- Key: A minor or C major
- Harmony: restrained verse loop that resolves into a warmer chorus lift
- Palette: grooved drums, warm bass, clean keys, muted guitar plucks, light synth top
- Finish level: full demo arrangement with believable section contrast
- Deliverable: full stereo bounce, aligned stems, section map, and Logic import notes
